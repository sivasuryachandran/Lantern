The gap

Three places a concern can go quiet.

Care is full of short conversations. The words and the answers can end up in different places.

Said in passing

A patient mentions something to whoever is nearby. It may never reach the chart, or reach it reworded.

Confirmed somewhere

A nurse or doctor answers out loud. Nothing shows later who confirmed what, or on which version of the chart.

Lost at shift change

The next shift inherits an open question without an owner who agreed to take it.

How Lantern thinks

Agents notice. People decide. Code enforces.

Agents notice

intake flags a possible discrepancy and quotes the patient exactly. care-coordinator carries the case to the doctor, citing where each fact came from. Neither can change the chart.

People decide

A nurse confirms the conflict. Only a verified doctor's decision changes the simulated chart, and the patient gets the doctor's own words.

Code enforces

A small record service checks the quote, the role, the room, the thread and the chart version. It is the only thing that says "resolved" or speaks to a patient.

Built on Switch

Teammates in the room, not a chatbot in a corner.

Switch puts AI agents into Mattermost rooms next to the people. Lantern uses that for the teamwork, and keeps isolation as room membership, not as a prompt.
