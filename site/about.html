About

Why we built Lantern.

Concerns are often spoken before they are charted. We wanted to see whether a small group of AI teammates and a strict record could keep a patient's words, a clinician's answer and the next shift's owner together, without letting an agent make the clinical call.

Lantern is

  • A shared record of concerns, decisions and handoffs that sits beside a chart.
  • A workflow demo built on Switch, with two AI agents that notice and route.
  • A strict record service where only a verified doctor's decision changes the simulated chart.
  • Fictional data from end to end.

Lantern isn't

  • An electronic health record, or clinical software. It is not for clinical use.
  • HIPAA-ready, and it gives no medical advice.
  • A replacement for documentation tools such as Abridge or Epic. Lantern sits beside them, not in place of them.
  • Reviewed by a clinician. We prepared the questions for a nurse review; it has not happened yet.
