How it works

One concern, three rooms, one confirmed answer.

Follow Case 17 from a passing remark at the bedside to a decision, a patient answer and a named owner. Scroll: each step lights a lantern.

1
patient-101

Said in passing

A patient mentions it at the bedside tablet.

"Can't wait for breakfast. Dr. Lee said it's fine now that my procedure moved."

The chart says diet = NPO (v3). record-bot, plain code, forwards the message to the staff room. No agent is a member of the patient room.

2
nurse-station

An AI teammate notices

intake flags a possible discrepancy.

POSSIBLE DISCREPANCY

The card quotes the patient exactly next to the active order. The service accepts it only if the quote is an exact piece of the original message, then opens Case 17 and tells the patient their care team is checking.

3
nurse-station · Case 17 thread

A nurse confirms

A person says it is real.

CONFIRM Case 17

Until a nurse replies, it stays a possible discrepancy. Nothing reaches a doctor on an agent's say-so alone.

4
doctor-review

With receipts

care-coordinator carries the case into the doctor's room.

✓ VERIFIED

It gets chart facts from a lookup tool that logs every call. Under each cited fact, record-bot posts a card built from that log: ✓ verified, or ⚠ when there is no matching lookup or the chart has moved on.

5
doctor-review · Case 17 thread

A clinician decides

Dr. Lee places the new order. She replies in the case thread.

DECIDE Case 17 v3 diet=regular

The service verifies author, role, room, thread, edit status and chart version, then bumps the simulated chart v3 → v4. The patient receives her words verbatim, with "Following up: nurse-ana". Any other attempt is refused and logged.

6
nurse-station

The next shift owns it

Shift change.

GIVEN HANDOFF ACCEPT Case 17

The outgoing nurse records what was given and what is still open. The incoming nurse accepts. The patient's owner line becomes "Following up: nurse-ben".

Trust model

Six kinds of record. Never merged.

What a patient said, what an agent drafted and what a person confirmed are stored apart, so the timeline always shows who said what.

⚠ STALE

No matching lookup, the wrong value, the wrong entry or version, or a chart that has moved on: none of these get a check mark. Anything an agent says without a citation is shown as unverified interpretation.

What breaks without Switch

Three things nothing else was doing.

Nothing carries the case

Without Switch there is no teammate in the doctor's room to summarize the evidence and answer from the live chart.

Nothing keeps agents out

Isolation is room membership. No agent is a member of any patient room, and one patient's account cannot open another's.

Nothing makes the agent accountable

The lookup tool is plain code that logs every call. The receipt cards come from that log.
