# Nurse review call (15 minutes, book before Thursday; Wednesday night is better)

Use a nurse who is not on the team. Everything shown is fictional. Do not ask for, or accept, real patient details.
Record the date, name or role, and anything you quote only with their permission.

Walk through the Case 17 timeline, then ask:

1. Is "a patient repeats something a clinician said that isn't on the chart yet" a real situation you see?
2. Who would you expect to check it, and in what order (nurse, then doctor)? Does the order of rooms match?
3. At shift change, is "accepting" a follow-up different from being told about it? What would you want to see?
4. Is recording a given dose separately from the order how you think about it?
5. What would make this unusable on a real ward? What is the first thing you would change?
6. Is anything on screen wrong, misleading or unsafe-looking?

Paste a short quote and what changed because of it into the README under "Nurse review".

Ask for permission to quote them by role (for example "charge nurse, 9 years") in the README and in the last 10 seconds of the video. Ask which sentence best sums up whether the shift-change handoff matches what they do today.
