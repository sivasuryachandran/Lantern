# B-roll prompts (for an external video generator)

Atmospheric cutaways only, to sit between the real screen recordings in `docs/DEMO_SCRIPT.md`. Generated footage shows no UI, no chart and no agent output. **It never stands in for a real run**: every Case 17 scene in the video stays a recording of the real Switch agents, and the video says (on screen or in the voice-over) that the cutaways are generated. All data and people are fictional.

## Style (apply to every shot)

Cinematic, quiet, warm, hopeful. Night shift in a calm modern hospital ward. Soft practical lighting, warm amber lamp glow against deep navy shadows, shallow depth of field, slow dolly moves, 24fps film look, subtle grain. No visible logos, no real brands, no identifiable real people, no on-screen text in the generated footage. 16:9.
